Jason Chen

I am a fourth year undergraduate student at the University of Southern California working with Prof. Daniel Seita and Prof. Gaurav S. Sukhatme. I'm majoring in Computer Science.

My current research focuses on a data-driven approach to embodied intelligence that generates synthetic data from previously collected datasets for deployment in offline reinforcement learning, planning, and imitation learning.

I am interested in developing data-efficient algorithms and policies for multi-modal perception that harness vision, touch, audio, and language for fine-grained and effective manipulation.

I am currently also getting an accelerated master's degree in Computer Science at USC.

Looking for PhD programs starting in Fall 2027!
